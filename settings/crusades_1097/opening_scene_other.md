The water in the skin tastes of goat and the road from Nicaea tastes of dust. You have been walking
behind Bohemond's Normans for three days, because the vanguard is where the food is, and because
everyone says the Turks won't dare come near the knights.

This evening nobody is saying that.

A Norman sergeant with a broken nose is going along the line of camp followers, pulling out anyone
who looks like they can hold something sharp. He stops at you and squints.

"You. Can you use that, or is it for show?" He jerks his chin at the ridgeline, where something is
moving against the sunset that is not goats. "We dig a ditch round the baggage tonight. Anyone who
digs eats. Anyone who runs, I find."
