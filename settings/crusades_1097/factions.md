# Factions — Crusades 1097 Setting Pack

## The Crusader Princes' Council
The loose, fractious leadership of the expedition - Godfrey, Raymond, Bohemond, Robert of Normandy,
Robert of Flanders, and others, none formally subordinate to any other. `crusader_leaders_regard`
tracks Bohemond's standing within this council specifically; decisions in practice get made through
shifting coalitions, not a clean chain of command.

## The Byzantine Empire
Emperor Alexios I's court and administration - the expedition's essential (and resented) logistics
base, and the party to the oath of fealty most crusader leaders swore before crossing into Anatolia.
`byzantine_relations` tracks this directly; it is both a genuine strategic asset and a genuine
long-term liability, since fulfilling the oath and pursuing personal territorial ambition are
directly in tension for a character like Bohemond.

## The Church and the Legate's Authority
Adhemar of Le Puy's spiritual leadership over the expedition, and the wider papal sanction behind
the whole enterprise. `church_favor` reflects both.

## The Rank and File
The ordinary pilgrims, soldiers, and camp-followers of the wider host, beyond any one leader's
personal contingent - a real political constituency in their own right, prone to both extraordinary
religious fervor and real desperation as supply and morale fluctuate.

## The Seljuk and Regional Muslim Powers
Antioch's defenders, the wider Seljuk political world (including Kerbogha of Mosul), and other
regional powers (Fatimid Egypt among them) with their own, often competing, interests in how the
crusade's advance plays out - not a monolith, and a plausible source of the kind of local alliance
or negotiation that pure holy-war framing tends to obscure.
