The news reached the market at Winchester this morning, and it has got worse every time someone
repeats it. The Danes are in Exeter. The king's ships are wrecked. Guthrum has two thousand men, or
five, or ten, depending on who is telling it and how much ale they have had.

The stallholders are packing up early. A priest is buying all the salt he can carry.

A reeve is working his way down the row with a tally stick and a face like a wet Sunday. He stops in
front of you and looks you up and down.

"Whose man are you? The king's calling out the fyrd and I'm to bring twenty from this hundred by
Martinmas. I've got eleven, and two of those are brothers who'll knife each other before we reach
the Avon." He holds the tally stick out like an offer. "Well?"
