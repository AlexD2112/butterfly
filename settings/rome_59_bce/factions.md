# Factions — Rome 59 BCE Setting Pack

## The First Triumvirate
The private alliance between Caesar, Pompey, and Crassus - not a legal office, not public
knowledge in its full extent, and entirely dependent on the three men's continued mutual interest.
`triumvirate_trust` is the resource that tracks this specifically, separate from `senate_standing`,
because the alliance's health and Caesar's standing with the actual Senate move independently and
sometimes in opposite directions.

## The Optimates
The conservative senatorial faction, led in spirit (though not formal office) by Cato, defending
the traditional prerogatives of the Senate against what they see as increasingly dangerous
concentrations of individual power. Bibulus's obstructionism is optimate strategy in miniature.
Not a monolith - individual optimates can be courted, isolated, or reconciled with even while the
faction as a whole stays hostile.

## The Populares
The broad current of popular-assembly-focused politics Caesar himself is associated with -
Clodius operates in this space too, but with his own agenda, not as Caesar's instrument. Real power
here, but volatile and harder to fully control than senatorial politics.

## The Equites
The wealthy business/financial class, a constituency Crassus in particular has deep ties to and
that cares primarily about provincial tax-farming contracts and stable conditions for commerce -
a useful lever for `treasury` and `patrician_network` that isn't quite the same as either the
optimates or the populares.

## Client Kings and Foreign Powers
Rome's network of dependent foreign rulers and the wider Mediterranean world beyond direct Roman
control - relevant to `foreign_reputation` and a plausible venue for the kind of quiet diplomacy
that doesn't require Senate approval.
