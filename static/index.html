Butterfly

Change history.

A game master narrates the story and plays every character you meet. When you attempt something risky, it's resolved with an actual dice roll against your character's stats, not just narrated however it seems to fit in the moment.

Four settings so far: Leon Trotsky in Petrograd in 1917, Alfred the Great fighting off the Vikings in 876, Julius Caesar in Rome, and Bohemond of Taranto on the First Crusade. Play the setting's main historical character, or build your own from scratch. If you end up making different choices than history did, the game keeps track of that as a real divergence, not just a line of flavor text.
