How It Works

A Game Master For Everything

An AI narrates every scene and speaks for every character you meet, reacting to whatever you actually try rather than following a fixed script.

The Dice Decide the Risky Moments

Ordinary conversation just plays out. Anything genuinely risky is resolved with a roll against your own character's stats, not narrated however it happens to feel in the moment.

History Remembers

Step outside the record and the game logs it as a real divergence — part of your character's permanent story from then on, not a passing line of flavor text.

Alone, or Together

Play solo at your own pace, or join a shared server where everyone's choices shape the same unfolding world alongside yours.
